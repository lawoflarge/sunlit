What you are getting

Sunlit is an iPhone app that computes the position of the sun, the moon and the galactic centre of the Milky Way. It is free to install and free to use for today's date at your current location. A single non consumable in app purchase, Sunlit Pro, unlocks any date, saved places, the moon, the Milky Way, annual paths, the measured horizon, eclipses, widgets, notifications and export. There is no subscription and there is no second product.

The purchase

Payment, refunds and family sharing are handled by Apple under the terms of the App Store, not by us. Apple's standard licence applies in addition to this page and can be read at apple.com/legal.

What the numbers are, and are not

Sunlit computes solar position with the NREL Solar Position Algorithm and lunar position with a truncated ELP-2000/82 series. Those figures are accurate to the tolerances stated in the App Store listing.

The UV index and the irradiance figures are clear sky models, not measurements. They estimate what a cloudless sky would deliver. Cloud is not modelled. Do not use them as a substitute for sun protection advice, for a UV meter, or for any decision where being wrong would harm you.

Rise, set and twilight times assume a flat horizon unless you have traced your own skyline in the app. Nothing here is suitable for navigation, aviation, maritime use, or any purpose where a life depends on the number.
