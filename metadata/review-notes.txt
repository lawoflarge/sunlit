Sunlit computes the position of the sun, the moon and the centre of the Milky Way
on the device, for any date and any place. No account, no sign in, no demo
credentials. Launching goes straight to the Sky view.

NO ACCOUNT OR NETWORK NEEDED TO TEST
Every figure is computed locally. In aeroplane mode every calculation, curve and
table still works, including the offline city search over 34,106 entries. Two
things use a network, both Apple's: the map tiles, and the time zone of a pin
dropped somewhere new. What is drawn on the map is computed locally.

THE FOUR VIEWS are tabs at the bottom: Sky (home, the gradient is drawn from the
real solar altitude, drag the scrubber to move through the day), AR (camera with
the sun and moon paths and the solstice and equinox curves), Map (sunrise and
sunset azimuth rays and a shadow projection), Data (twilights, moon calendar,
eclipses, annual curve, export). Place and date live in a shared header and
govern all four.

CAMERA PERMISSION AND GRACEFUL DEGRADATION
The AR view is the only feature using the camera and asks when that tab is first
opened. NSCameraUsageDescription is localised in all ten languages. If permission
is refused or later revoked, the view does not fail: it drops the camera layer
and draws the same paths over the app's own sky gradient. If location is refused,
a place can still be chosen from the offline list or by dropping a pin, so the
app stays fully testable.

THE FREE TIER
Today, at the current location, is free forever, in all four views: sun position,
compass, AR sun path, sunrise, sunset, all three twilights, golden and blue hour,
solar noon, day length, shadow length, and the UV and irradiance figures. Nothing
in that list is time limited and there is no trial. The paywall states what stays
free before it states what costs money.

UV INDEX AND IRRADIANCE ARE CLEAR SKY MODELS
They are not measurements and are never presented as such. UV comes from the
solar zenith angle, an embedded latitude and season ozone climatology, the site
elevation and the earth to sun distance. Irradiance is the Haurwitz clear sky
model with the Erbs correlation splitting direct and diffuse. Every place these
appear carries a visible clear sky caption, and the store description says the
same in every language. Cloud is not modelled and the app says so.

ACCURACY CLAIMS
Solar position uses the NREL Solar Position Algorithm, accurate to 0.0003
degrees. Lunar position uses a truncated ELP-2000/82 series, accurate to about 10
arcseconds. Rise and set come from a numerical solver, so high latitudes are
handled and polar day and night are reported explicitly rather than as an
invented time. These are the only accuracy claims made, each verified against
published reference values.

PRIVACY
The app collects nothing: no account, no email, no analytics, no crash reporter,

Saved places and the measured skyline are written inside the app container and
